MapleCompass Setup

Before you start: your account

MapleCompass is a third-party tool, not made or approved by Nexon. Nexon's terms forbid some third-party programs, and a penalty in Classic World applies to your whole account, MapleStory included. Using MapleCompass is at your own risk.

To keep that risk low, MapleCompass only looks at the screen:

  • It captures the minimap title (and, if you choose, the EXP bar) the same way a screenshot does, and draws its own window.
  • It never reads or changes the game's memory or files, and never injects anything into it.
  • It never presses keys or clicks for you, and automates nothing.

Find the game window

Set up the game

  • Keep the minimap open, with its title showing. MapleCompass reads the street name and map name at the top of the minimap.
  • Play in windowed or borderless mode. Screen capture can't see exclusive fullscreen, and the panel can only dock beside a window.

Mark the minimap title

Draw or adjust a box around the two lines of the minimap title: the street name and the map name. Leave a little of the blue panel around them.

Drag to draw a box, or use Arrow keys to move it and Alt+Arrow to resize. Hold Shift for larger steps. Enter tests the box; Esc cancels.

Long map names are read whole, even if the title is short now.

To see how fast you level, drag a box around the EXP figures on the EXP bar at the bottom of the game, like EXP. 160 [3.81%]. Take in the number and the percentage in brackets.

Help & Setup

Drag the panel by its header to move it, Inside or Detached; Docked panels stay beside the game. Placement cycles through Inside, Docked Right, Docked Left, and Detached. The magnifier at the top of the panel, or the search shortcut, finds a mob and the maps it spawns on; pick one to make it your Hunting Target, and the panel shows the way there until you arrive. To see what an item is worth, hover it in the game until its tooltip shows and press the item card shortcut: its card shows what a shop pays for it, what players pay for it, and where it comes from and what it's for. Its name opens its MeowDB page in your browser, as the MeowDB shortcut does at once.

Map Summary: 6+ means a party of six or more players. Share is a mob's share of all spawns on the Map.
