MapleCompass setup

Before you start: your account

MapleCompass is a third-party tool, not made or approved by Nexon. Nexon's terms forbid some third-party programs, and a penalty in Classic World applies to your whole account, MapleStory included. Using MapleCompass is at your own risk.

To keep that risk low, MapleCompass only looks at the screen:

  • It captures the minimap title the same way a screenshot does, and draws its own window.
  • It never reads or changes the game's memory or files, and never injects anything into it.
  • It never presses keys or clicks for you, and automates nothing.

Find the game window

Set up the game

  • Keep the minimap open, with its title showing. MapleCompass reads the street name and map name at the top of the minimap.
  • Play in windowed or borderless mode. Screen capture can't see exclusive fullscreen, and the panel can only dock beside a window.

Mark the minimap title

Drag a box around the two lines of the minimap title: the street name and the map name. Leave a little of the blue panel around them.

Press Esc to cancel. Long map names are read whole, even if the title is short now.
