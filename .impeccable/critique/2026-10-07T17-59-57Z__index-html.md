## Overall impression

A coherent, quiet companion with a sound visual foundation. The weakest moments are the transition from successful setup into play and recovery when recognition or a shortcut fails. Improve those moments before adding visual expression.

## What is working

- **The information order supports quick consultation.** The Map Name stays dominant while hunting facts and Hidden Portals are grouped beneath it.
- **Uncertainty and provenance are honest.** Question marks, Candidate notes, dated Saved Copies, and attribution give the player useful context.
- **Setup verifies its own result.** Finish is disabled until recognition succeeds, while failed reads preserve Mark again and explain how to retry.

## Priority issues

### 1. [P1] Essential controls are missing from the setup handoff

**Evidence:** the final setup step teaches re-setup but not collapse, edit, or placement ([setup.html](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/setup.html:80), [src/setup.ts](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/setup.ts:104)). The normal footer teaches only opening the source page.

**Why it matters:** a player can finish successfully and still not know how to reclaim game space or move the panel.

**Fix:** show a concise handoff using the current collapse, edit, and placement bindings; make it retrievable in the setup window. Include placement cycling in edit-mode guidance. Keep the normal overlay click-through.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable onboard`.

### 2. [P1] Mandatory marking requires a pointer; step changes lose keyboard context

**Evidence:** [src/region.ts](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/region.ts:25) implements mouse dragging; keyboard support only cancels with Escape. Keyboard activation of Next advanced setup but left focus on BODY. [src/setup.ts](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/setup.ts:33) hides the old step without moving focus; changing results have no live-status semantics.

**Why it matters:** a keyboard-only player cannot complete mandatory marking, and assistive technology lacks a reliable new-step/result signal.

**Fix:** provide keyboard-adjustable selection or accessible coordinate controls within MapleCompass's own window. Focus the new step heading and announce important result/status changes. These controls must act only on the companion's Title Region selection.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit`.

**Limit:** mouse-only handlers and missing semantics are source-confirmed; a native screen-reader session was not tested.

### 3. [P2] Candidate rows hide the facts needed to distinguish Maps

**Evidence:** two specimen rows measured 414px and 391px inside 352px of available space. [src/main.ts](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/main.ts:161) places IDs first and portal counts last; [src/styles.css](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/styles.css:64) forces one-line ellipsis.

**Why it matters:** unfamiliar IDs remain visible while useful distinctions disappear. Cycling through full Map data works, but interrupts comparison.

**Fix:** lead with player-visible differences, make IDs secondary, and allow a controlled second line. Preserve the existing hotkey selection model.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`.

### 4. [P2] Recovery guidance becomes weaker during normal play

**Evidence:** “MeowDB data unavailable,” “Map recognition unavailable,” and “Reading the minimap title…” lack next steps ([src/main.ts](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/main.ts:185), [src/main.ts](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/main.ts:288)). Hotkey recovery in setup asks for editing settings.json and restarting without giving its location ([src/setup.ts](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/setup.ts:108)).

**Why it matters:** players must infer whether to wait, reopen the minimap, mark again, reconnect, or repair a binding.

**Fix:** add brief, condition-specific recovery guidance and applicable retry information. Show the current setup shortcut only when usable; provide a settings location or accessible binding-recovery route in the ordinary setup window.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

### 5. [P2] Supporting text is too small or faint for easy consultation

**Evidence:** the browser confirmed 10px portal tags. The 11px footer foreground on the 82%-opaque panel yields about 3.24:1 contrast when composited over a white backdrop ([src/styles.css](C:/Users/itech/.t3/worktrees/maplecompass/t3code-eae29f9b/src/styles.css:184)). This is a bright-background calculation, not a claim about every game scene.

**Why it matters:** the routine source-page hint and required attribution are subdued; portal classifications are very small.

**Fix:** improve footer foreground/backing contrast and raise tag text modestly, while keeping both subordinate to the Map Name. Check them over representative dark and bright game fixtures.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`.
